## About Me

I am a postdoc researcher at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html) working with [Prof. Konrad Schindler](https://prs.igp.ethz.ch/group/people/person-detail.schindler.html). Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. degree at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch), advised by [Prof. Devis Tuia](https://people.epfl.ch/devis.tuia) and [Prof. Antoine Bosselut](https://atcbosselut.github.io/), and my master's degree at [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/), working with [Prof. Zhenzhong Chen](https://zhenzhong-chen.github.io/index.html). I am interested in multimodal learning and its application to geo-aware artificial intelligence.
